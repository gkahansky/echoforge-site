How long does turnaround actually take? expand_more
Accessibility audit reports are delivered within 24 hours of order. Free sample reports are faster — typically 10–20 minutes.
How does the free sample work? expand_more
Enter your URL and email to receive a sample WCAG score, issue category summary, and contrast highlights. No credit card required.
How do you ensure quality? expand_more
EchoForge combines structured workflows, automation, and review logic to produce clean, publication-ready deliverables. The free sample lets you verify quality before purchasing, and the paid audits are designed to give you clear next steps rather than generic output.

Every audit checks your pages against WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA criteria — colour contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, focus management, alt text, and more. Findings are grouped by severity with affected selectors called out, so your developer knows exactly what to fix.

If the output doesn't meet the quality standard described, contact us within 7 days and we'll reprocess or refund. We offer a free sample specifically so you can evaluate quality before committing.

Your website URL and email are used solely to generate your audit report. We do not share, sell, or retain your data beyond what's needed for delivery.
